# MythOS

A full desktop experience that lives in your browser. Grand Opening edition.

## Features

- A polished first-run setup with light and dark looks and your own accent colour
- Sign in with Microsoft or keep a private local account, with your own name and picture
- A desktop with draggable icons, a taskbar, a Start menu and a quick settings panel
- Windows you can move, resize, snap and minimize, just like a real PC
- Files app with folders, drag and drop, and a working Recycle Bin you can restore from
- A Recycle Bin icon on the desktop that shows an empty bin, and turns into a full bin when there are deleted items to restore
- Live wallpapers, including the Bloom collection, and your own uploads
- Personalization: themes, accent colours, fonts, rounded corners and glass effects
- Built-in apps for everyday use, including messages to chat with friends
- Right-click menus everywhere, and your settings are saved for next time

More coming soon.
