# Windows 11

### Grand Opening

A full desktop, right in your browser. No install, no downloads. Open it and it's yours.

## Features

- **Your own desktop:** drag icons and apps anywhere, pin favorites to the taskbar and Start, and open it on any device.
- **Sign in your way:** use a local account, or sign up with Microsoft.
- **Customize everything:** live wallpapers, your own pictures and videos, fonts, accent colors, light and dark mode.
- **Files:** upload music, videos, pictures and documents, and they sort themselves into the right tabs.
- **Music:** a full player with queue, shuffle, repeat, visualizer and cover art, plus search for full songs.
- **YouTube:** watch inside the OS, with several servers that switch automatically.
- **Messages:** create chat servers and talk with friends in near real time.
- **Run your own apps:** drop in an HTML file or a zip and it becomes an app on your desktop. Games work too.
- **Settings:** search any setting, tab cloaking, a panic key, accessibility options and more.
- **Games:** controller support built in.

Welcome to Windows 11.
